The same model reported in the paper

These are saved outputs from the Dual Radar ZB=2 model: the epoch 300 XY-ODA diffusion generator and the manuscript's Geometry-consistent learned decoder (soft_control mixed10).

The original 1000-index schedule uses 999 adjacent reverse updates. This demonstration does not use DDIM50, respaced sampling or the alternative integer-aware decoder.
